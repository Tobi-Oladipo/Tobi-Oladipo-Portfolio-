    Predictive modeling

    Healthcare Analytics Research (CLABSI Prediction)

    • Analyzed healthcare data to identify risk factors associated with hospital-acquired bloodstream infections.
    • Used Python and statistical modeling techniques to improve predictive performance and support evidence-based recommendations.
    DataTexas Children’s Hospital, in a graduate practicum with a six-person team and faculty mentors.
    QuestionWill a patient develop a central line-associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI) within the next three days? True or False.
    Data problemsA heavily imbalanced target, 239,605 missing values and outliers.
    Four model typesWeighted neural network, bagged decision trees, support vector machine, XGBoost.
    EvaluationStratified k-fold cross-validation; precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC; decision and cost curves, since a missed infection costs more than a false alarm.

    Python · scikit-learn · XGBoost
